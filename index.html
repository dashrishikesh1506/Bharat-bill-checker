💡 Understanding Your Bill Breakdown

Indian utility bills are composed of 4 key components. Here is what each means:

Fixed / Demand Charges
Monthly maintenance fee based on your Sanctioned Load (kW). Payable even if usage is 0 units.
Telescopic Slab Energy Charge
Unit price rises with consumption (e.g. 0-100 units @ ₹3.50, 101-200 @ ₹5.50, >200 @ ₹8.00).
FPPPA / FAC (Fuel Surcharge)
Cost of coal/gas adjustment passed onto consumers monthly. Often causes sudden unexpected bill jumps!
Electricity Duty & Taxes
State tax (usually 5% to 15%) levied on total energy charges by your state government.

⚠️ Top 3 Bill Overcharge Traps in India

1. Delayed Meter Reading (45-Day Trap)
If the reader comes after 40-45 days instead of 30, your units cross into high tariff slabs, unfairly inflating your bill!
2. 'Door Lock' Average Billing
If the reader claims your house was locked, they bill on provisional inflated estimates instead of actual meter units.
3. Missing Security Deposit Interest
DISCOMs hold your security deposit and MUST pay 6-7% bank interest credited directly to your bill each year!

⚖️ How to Dispute an Incorrect Bill

Under Section 42 of the Indian Electricity Act 2003, every consumer has the legal right to challenge unfair bills through this 3-tier system:

Tier 1: DISCOM Sub-Division
Submit written complaint or call 1912. Get a formal complaint reference number.
Tier 2: CGRF Forum
If DISCOM doesn't fix it within 30 days, escalate to Consumer Grievance Redressal Forum.
Tier 3: Electricity Ombudsman
Appointed by the State Commission. Ombudsman orders are legally binding on power companies!
